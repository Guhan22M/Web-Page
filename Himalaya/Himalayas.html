About Us

At Himalaya Wellness, we are deeply committed to improving health and well-being through the power of nature. Founded in 1930, our journey began with a single-minded passion to bring the timeless wisdom of Ayurveda to the world. For over nine decades, we have been dedicated to blending traditional knowledge with modern science to create safe, effective, and innovative herbal products. Our mission is to enhance the lives of people everywhere with wellness solutions that are rooted in nature and backed by rigorous research. With a portfolio spanning personal care, nutrition, pharmaceuticals, and animal health, Himalaya Wellness is a global leader in providing holistic healthcare solutions. We believe in the power of nature to heal, nurture, and rejuvenate. From sourcing the finest herbs to implementing sustainable farming practices, we are committed to maintaining the purity and potency of our ingredients. Our products are crafted with care, free from harmful chemicals, and created with the well-being of both people and the planet in mind.
